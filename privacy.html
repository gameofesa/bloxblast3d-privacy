Privacy Policy

Blox Blast 3D: Block Puzzle · Last updated: October 31, 2026

This policy explains what information Blox Blast 3D ("the game", "we") collects and how it is used. Developer: Enes Kömü. Contact: komuenes969@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (scores, adventure levels, coins, boosters, unlocked block skins and themes, settings such as sound, vibration and language) is stored only on your device. We do not run our own servers and we do not receive this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, only if you allow tracking; the advertising ID on Android), IP address and approximate location derived from it, ad interactions, app usage (for example app launches), and crash and performance data of the ad SDK. This data is not linked to your identity by us. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

On iOS the game asks for permission before the advertising identifier can be used for tracking (App Tracking Transparency). In the EEA, UK and Switzerland a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) lets you choose how your data is used for ads; you can change your choice any time in the game under Settings › Privacy Options.

Purchases (Apple App Store and Google Play)

In-app purchases (coin packs and the "No Ads – 30 Days" pass) are one-time purchases and do not renew automatically. They are processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play; we never see your payment details. The game only receives a confirmation that a purchase was completed so it can be delivered on your device.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location. The game does not require an account.

Children

Blox Blast 3D is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data retention and your choices

Deleting the game removes all game data from your device. You can reset your advertising identifier or limit ad tracking in your device settings (iOS: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking). We do not sell your personal information.

Changes

If we update this policy we will publish the new version on this page with a new date.
