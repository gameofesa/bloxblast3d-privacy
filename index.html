Frequently asked questions

Is my progress saved?
Yes. Your progress is saved automatically on your device. Deleting the game also deletes the saved progress.

How does "No Ads – 30 Days" work?
It is a one-time purchase that removes ads for 30 days and adds 1,000 coins. It does not renew automatically. You can buy it again whenever you like; each purchase adds 30 more days.

A purchase did not arrive.
Make sure you are connected to the internet and restart the game; pending purchases are delivered automatically. You can also use "Restore purchases" in the Shop. If it still does not appear, email us.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?
On iOS: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland: in the game under Settings › Privacy Options.
